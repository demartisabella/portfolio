## Project Overview

Designed a production planning and control system for an agricultural manufacturer to improve visibility throughout the production process. The solution transformed an Excel-based scheduling tool into a decision-support system capable of tracking production yields, estimating raw material requirements, identifying shortages before delivery, and supporting proactive production planning.

## Problem

The existing planning process focused primarily on production dates while providing limited visibility into material losses throughout the production cycle. As products progressed through sowing, seedling, germination, and nursery stages, untracked yield losses increased the risk of late shortage detection, resulting in reactive replanning and delayed customer orders—particularly for high-volume requests.

## Engineering Approach

Developed a production planning model that combined process mapping, production traceability, yield modeling, and decision-support tools to strengthen operational planning.

The proposed system incorporated:

- Production traceability across every manufacturing stage
- Stage-specific control points to monitor production progress
- Yield-loss modeling to estimate required initial production quantities
- Automated shortage prediction based on projected process yields
- Visual risk alerts for production orders
- Operational KPIs for future monitoring of production performance

Rather than replacing the company's existing workflow, the solution enhanced its current Excel platform with engineering calculations, standardized production controls, and decision-support capabilities.


## Key Results

- Developed a yield-based planning model that calculates the initial production quantity required to fulfill customer orders by accounting for cumulative losses across three production stages (seedbed, germination, and nursery).
- Demonstrated the model by calculating that a 1,000-unit customer order would require an initial production of 1,200 units, resulting in an estimated 1,007 deliverable units after expected process losses, ensuring full order fulfillment with a small safety margin.
- Redesigned the planning tool to incorporate 10 automated planning formulas, including yield calculations, shortage forecasting, excess inventory estimation, order fulfillment tracking, and color-coded production risk alerts.
- Designed a production control framework with 3 stage-specific checkpoints, 3 visual risk levels (Green, Yellow, Red), and 6 operational KPIs to monitor fulfillment, process losses, delays, shortages, production costs, and profitability.
- Proposed a solution requiring only 36 hours of implementation effort with an estimated implementation cost of ₡55,963, leveraging the company's existing Excel platform without additional software or infrastructure investment.

## Impact

The proposed system shifts production planning from reactive scheduling to proactive operational control. By integrating production traceability, yield-based planning, automated shortage detection, and standardized decision rules, the solution enables earlier identification of production risks, improves planning accuracy, and establishes the operational data foundation required for continuous improvement and future forecasting initiatives.

## Lessons Learned

This project reinforced that effective production planning is driven by accurate operational data rather than schedules alone. I learned how to model process yields, translate customer demand into production requirements, and design control systems that provide early visibility into operational risks. It also strengthened my ability to develop practical, low-cost decision-support tools by combining process analysis, inventory planning, and production control principles to improve operational decision-making.
