## Research Overview

Once operational procedures had been standardized, the next challenge was eliminating manual data handling. This research presents the design and implementation of a Microsoft Power Platform ecosystem that digitizes production data collection, automates OEE calculations, and delivers real-time manufacturing analytics.

## Problem

Production information was scattered across paper forms and multiple Excel workbooks, requiring manual consolidation after each production cycle. The process consumed weeks of engineering effort, delayed decision-making, and increased the likelihood of transcription errors.

## Solution

A digital workflow was developed using:

- Microsoft Lists
- Power Apps
- SharePoint
- Power Query
- Power BI

to automate production data collection, transformation, validation, and visualization.

## Key Contributions

- Designed a complete low-code manufacturing data pipeline.
- Eliminated manual spreadsheet consolidation.
- Standardized production data capture.
- Enabled real-time OEE monitoring.
- Reduced reporting effort from weeks to minutes.

## Engineering Value

The project demonstrates that low-code technologies can provide practical Industry 4.0 solutions without requiring expensive Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES), making digital transformation accessible to universities and small-to-medium manufacturers.
