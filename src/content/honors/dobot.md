## Overview

This certification represents completion of comprehensive training on Dobot robotic systems, covering programming, operation, and practical applications of collaborative robots in educational and industrial contexts. The training emphasized hands-on experience with robot control, path programming, and automation workflows.

## What It Represents

- Practical robotics programming and control
- Understanding of collaborative robot (cobot) applications
- Automation workflow design and implementation
- Safety protocols for robotic systems operation
- Integration of robotics into manufacturing processes

## Details

- **Issuer:** Tecnológico de Costa Rica, Campus San Carlos
- **Date:** October 24, 2025
- **Duration:** 6 hours
- **Instructors:** Ing. Daniel Campos M. & Saray Serrano (Dobot Exclusive Representative for Costa Rica)

## Why It Matters

This training expands my automation skillset beyond software platforms to include physical automation systems. Understanding robotic programming and operation complements my work in flexible manufacturing cells and Industry 4.0 initiatives, providing practical knowledge of how digital systems interface with physical automation equipment.
