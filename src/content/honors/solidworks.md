## Overview

The Certified SOLIDWORKS Associate (CSWA) certification validates fundamental competency in 3D CAD design, parametric modeling, and engineering design principles using industry-standard SOLIDWORKS software. This certification is recognized globally by engineering employers and demonstrates practical ability to create and modify 3D parts, assemblies, and technical drawings.

## What It Represents

- Proficiency in parametric 3D modeling and design intent
- Ability to create production-ready technical drawings with proper dimensioning and tolerancing
- Competency in assembly modeling and mate relationships
