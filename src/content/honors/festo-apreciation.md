## Overview

This official letter of appreciation from the School of Industrial Production Engineering recognizes my contribution to the restoration and commissioning of the FESTO flexible manufacturing cell at the PROTEC research building. The recognition highlights the technical commitment, teamwork, and excellence in managing manufacturing technologies that are fundamental to the school's research and academic capabilities.

## What It Represents

- Technical leadership in complex equipment restoration
- Collaborative problem-solving in multidisciplinary team environment
- Commitment to advancing institutional research infrastructure
- Hands-on expertise with FESTO manufacturing systems
- Professional excellence recognized by academic leadership

## Details

- **Issuer:** School of Industrial Production Engineering, TEC
- **Date:** February 13, 2025
- **Recognition Type:** Official Institutional Appreciation
- **Signed by:** Ing. Leonel Fonseca Retana, M.Sc. (Director) & Ing. Félix Badilla Murillo, Ph.D. (Coordinator)
- **Project:** FESTO Manufacturing Cell Restoration and Commissioning

## Why It Matters

This recognition validates my ability to work with advanced manufacturing equipment and contribute to research infrastructure development. The FESTO cell restoration was foundational to my subsequent FlexLAB research project, where I implemented digital OEE monitoring. The appreciation reflects the school's confidence in my technical capabilities and commitment to advancing Industry 4.0 initiatives at TEC.
