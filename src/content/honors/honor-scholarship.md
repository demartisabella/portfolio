## Overview

The Academic Honor Scholarship (Beca de Honor) is awarded by TEC's Scholarships and Social Management Department to students who demonstrate exceptional academic performance. This scholarship provides 100% exemption from tuition fees and represents formal recognition of placement among the institution's highest-achieving students.

## What It Represents

- Sustained academic excellence across rigorous engineering coursework
- Top-tier GPA performance
- Consistent high achievement in technical and analytical subjects
- Recognition by institutional academic standards
- 100% tuition exemption as merit-based reward

## Details

- **Issuer:** TEC Scholarships and Social Management Department
- **Period:** I Semester 2026 and II Semester 2025
- **Benefit:** 100% tuition exemption
- **Criteria:** Outstanding academic performance
- **Cumulative GPA:** 88.89/100

## Why It Matters

This scholarship validates my ability to excel in TEC's demanding Industrial Production Engineering curriculum while simultaneously leading research projects, coordinating international events, and developing industry partnerships. The consistent recognition (awarded for both II-2025 and I-2026) demonstrates sustained excellence rather than isolated achievement, reflecting strong time management, technical aptitude, and commitment to engineering education.
